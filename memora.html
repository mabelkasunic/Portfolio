The Brief

Overview

Memora is an AI-powered reading companion designed to help readers reconnect with the stories they love. It complements existing reading habits without changing where or how people read.

Challenge

Readers often lose track of characters, relationships and plot details between reading sessions. Searching for forgotten information can disrupt immersion and unintentionally expose readers to spoilers.

Solution

Memora transforms complex narratives into interactive character maps, visual timelines and searchable story knowledge. AI organizes and progressively reveals information based on reading progress, helping readers remember, reconnect and continue with confidence.
