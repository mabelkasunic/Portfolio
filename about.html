I think the way I design comes from how I live: curious, a little detail-obsessed, and always looking for the good stuff. That might be a waterfall at the end of a long hike, a Lego flower built piece by piece, or a perfect plate of seafood. Some days it’s the rush of a fresh run on the ski hill; others, it’s a book I can’t put down.
